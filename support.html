Common questions

How do I delete an intention? Tap the candle count at the top of the feed to open Your intentions, then tap Delete under the intention.

How do I light a past intention again? Open Your intentions and tap Start a new candle under it. The earlier candle keeps its lights.

How do I put out my candle early? Open Your intentions and tap Put out.

How do I change or hide my name? Open Your intentions and, under Settings, tap Name on intentions. You can choose to stay anonymous.

Why did my prayer not count? A prayer counts only when you stay with the candle for the full thirty seconds. Leaving the app puts it out.

How do I report something? On any intention, tap Report. You can also choose Hide this person's intentions.

How do I delete my account? Open Your intentions and, under Settings, choose Delete account. This removes your account, intentions and prayers.
